BINGSHO XP

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains what Bingsho XP collects and why.

1. What We Collect

  • Discord User ID
  • Discord Server (Guild) ID
  • Server configuration
  • XP and level information
  • Text activity used for XP calculations
  • Voice activity used for XP calculations
  • Related timestamps and progression statistics

2. Message Content

Bingsho XP may use Discord's Message Content Intent to detect message activity and award XP.

We do not intentionally store the full content of your messages as part of the XP system.

3. Voice Activity

Bingsho XP may process voice-state information to calculate voice XP.

We do not record, listen to, or store voice conversations.

4. How We Use Data

  • Calculate XP and levels
  • Provide leaderboards
  • Track progression
  • Save server settings
  • Operate the dashboard
  • Prevent abuse and troubleshoot issues

5. Data Sharing

Data may be processed by trusted infrastructure providers required to operate Bingsho XP, such as hosting, database, and authentication providers.

We do not sell your data.

6. Data Retention

We keep data for as long as reasonably necessary to provide Bingsho XP. Data may be deleted when it is no longer required or following a valid deletion request.

7. Data Deletion

To request deletion of data associated with Bingsho XP, contact the Bingsho XP team through the support/contact method provided on the dashboard.

8. Discord

Bingsho XP operates through Discord. Your use of Discord is also subject to Discord's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

9. Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated when our service or data practices change.

10. Contact

Questions or data requests can be sent through the support/contact method provided on the Bingsho XP dashboard.
